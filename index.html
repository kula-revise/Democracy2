The essay so far

Neither side could win, but a stalemate does not negotiate itself: leaders had to choose to act on it, and in 1990 De Klerk and Mandela did. Between 1990 and 1992 both sides gave up things nobody could make them give up, which is what political will looks like in practice.

Sentence two does a specific job. It defines your key term. The question is about political will, so you are telling the marker what you will count as will: a concession that could not have been forced. From here on, every example you use has to meet that test.
